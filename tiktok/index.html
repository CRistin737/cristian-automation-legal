Content Automation OS

Content Automation OS is a private desktop tool used only by its owner, Cristian Santos, to produce his own short educational videos and send them to his own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner writes a script and records the narration on his computer.
  2. The tool renders the final vertical video with captions, music and a cover.
  3. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and approves the requested permissions.
  4. The tool uploads each finished video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, video.upload). The owner opens TikTok, reviews the draft, chooses the privacy setting and posts it himself.

The tool is not offered to the public, does not access other people's accounts, and never reads followers, messages or analytics from TikTok.
