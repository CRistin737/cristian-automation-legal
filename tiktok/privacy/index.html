Privacy Policy

Content Automation OS · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Content Automation OS (the "App") is a private tool operated by Cristian Santos, resident in the Dominican Republic. It is used only by its owner to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account. Contact: poszysupport@gmail.com.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • When the owner signs in with TikTok: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), plus access and refresh tokens.
  • When a video is uploaded: the upload/publish ID and its processing status.

The App does not request or store passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, followers, messages, comments or analytics, and it never accesses accounts other than the owner's.

3. How we use it

Only to upload the owner's own finished videos to his TikTok inbox (and, if enabled, to post them with the privacy setting he chooses) and to check whether an upload succeeded. Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored in a file readable only by the App on a computer controlled by the owner, never in source code or logs, and all communication with TikTok uses HTTPS. Only the owner has access.

5. Retention

Tokens are kept while the connection is active and deleted when the owner disconnects the App or revokes access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Upload IDs and status are kept for at most 30 days.

6. Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of any data by writing to poszysupport@gmail.com. We follow Dominican Republic Law No. 172-13 on personal data protection. TikTok processes data under its own privacy policy.

7. Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with a new date.
